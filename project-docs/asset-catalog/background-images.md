## Batch reading

This is a phone-wallpaper grab-bag — mostly 9:16 portrait AI-art and macro-photo textures pulled from Pinterest/wallpaper sites — but a real house style hides inside it: warm impasto florals, red-and-gold ink marble, dusty-rose paper/plaster textures, and a strong parallel family of **midnight-blue-and-gold** regal pieces. That warm-rose + gold + regal-navy triad is exactly the CreativelyNanda soul palette (golden light, red-rose accents, heritage, poetry), so the collection genuinely can dress the whole site if curated. The true keepers are the ones with real depth and brand color — the crimson/gold petal and marble pieces (`download (15)`, `download (29)`, `petal`, `bloom`), the gold-lit night blooms (`download (40)`, `абстракция`), the regal navy-gold botanicals (`download (41)`, `download (45)`, `download (39)`), the pressed-rose-petal photo (`download (23)`), the soft sensual peony/dahlia macros (`download (30)`, `poetry-erasure`, `Ultra HD 4K Dark`), and the two narrative quilled-paper cherry-blossom pieces already wired to poetry pages. The retire pile is the generic phone-wallpaper filler: cheap rainbow gradients and "trendy abstract" swirls (`Abstract Colorful Design`, `50 Pink Aesthetic`, `TRENDY ABSTRACT`, `download (16)`, `download (21)`), plus exact **duplicates** of art files that already live elsewhere in `/assets/art` (`dancer`/`Abstract Dancer…Canvas Art`, `navy-floral`/`download (25)`, `download (30)`/`download (31)`). Two files carry visible marks — `bloom.jpg` has a "Meta AI" watermark, `download (23)` has a small artist signature — so treat those as second-choice unless cropped.

**Keepers (top tier):** `download (15)`, `download (29)`, `download (40)`, `абстракция`, `petal`, `bloom`*, `download (23)`, `download (30)`, `poetry-erasure-background`, `Ultra HD 4K Dark & Minimalist`, `download (41)`, `download (45)`, `download (39)`, `poetry-collection-background`, `Atmospheric reference for ALTERITAS`, `download (24)`.
