Only two files are actual photographs — both from the same NMB (Nelson Mandela Bay) Arts Festival set — and they are genuinely good: Nanda at the mic in striking black-and-multicolour **Ndebele/Xhosa-beaded traditional dress**, an accompanist in leopard-print with a djembe, keyboardist behind, warm venue light on an industrial brick-and-black-drape stage.
