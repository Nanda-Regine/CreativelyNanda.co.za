# Asset Catalog — `poetry-book/`

Folder: `public/assets/poetry-book/` — 19 images (every one opened and looked at, not read from filenames)
Subject: **"Inside Her Roses — A Poetry Collection"** by Nandawula Regine Kabali-Kagwa
Cover art: a stylized illustration of a Black woman seated cross-legged, long braids, cradling a spilling bouquet of red roses over her body, on deep matte black. Red-and-black is the book's signature palette (the launch theme was literally "Black & Red").

---

## Batch reading

This is a tight, on-brand set that is stronger than most author self-shoots. There are two visual registers: (1) crisp **product/packaging** shots of the physical black-and-red book — flatlays in a black gift tray, spine stacks, a café styling with poinsettia and cappuccino — and (2) genuinely lovely **golden-hour lifestyle** shots of young women reading the book outdoors under trees and against warm stone. The golden-light reading photos (`book-picture-2`, `book-picture-3`) are the jewels here: they already embody Nanda's "images breathe, warm veil, sensual/regal soul" design language and need almost no treatment. The illustrated cover itself is a superb recurring motif — regal, sensual, African, rose-red — and the six interior single-line chapter-divider drawings (Romance, Sensual, Life/implied, Depth, Empowering + the "Contents" and "Let The Journey Begin" pages) are a quiet goldmine for section dividers, watermarks, and margin ornaments across the whole site. The only weak links are utility/documentary shots (bulk shrink-wrapped stock, the shadowed front-page photo) which are proof-of-life, not hero material. Overall: enough here to carry a full "The Book" room plus decorative furniture for the rest of the House of Roses.

### Art-direction notes
- **Palette lock:** black + rose-red + warm skin/gold + magenta lip. This is the book's identity; carry it into the site's "book" room.
- **Two hero photos** (`book-picture-2`, `book-picture-3`) supply the warm-veil golden-hour breath the design language demands — use them full-bleed with text in the bright zones.
- **Extract the 6 line drawings** (romance, sensual, depth, empowering + the two implied Life/Personal if photographed elsewhere) as transparent PNGs — they are a ready-made system of section dividers, dropcaps, and margin ornaments.
- **`official-cover.jpg`** is the one canonical product/OG image; everything else is context around it.
- Utility/BTS (`books-bulk`, `book-launch-poster`, `book-front-page`, `book-cover-3`) belongs in timeline/proof/press, not as decorative backgrounds.
