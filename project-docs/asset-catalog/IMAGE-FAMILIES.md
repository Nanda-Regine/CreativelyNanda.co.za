# Image Families — `public/assets/nanda-portraits/`

*A decade of a woman photographed the way she lives: coast at dusk, bougainvillea in the hair, Xhosa beadwork under studio light, a black gown descending a staircase, a laptop open in the garden. These frames move from tenderness to regality and back — golden-hour softness, rose-red sensuality, navy-and-gold ceremony, terracotta earth. This is not a folder of pictures. It is a body of work with chapters, and what follows is its table of contents.*

**Scope:** 98 `.jpg` frames sorted into 12 soul-families plus an Archive section (documentary / non-portrait material) and a short list of flags. Two `.mp4` files are noted at the end but not catalogued. Where a family runs long, sub-vignettes are named inside it so the layout can breathe.
