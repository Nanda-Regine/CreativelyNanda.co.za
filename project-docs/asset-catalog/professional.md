# Asset Catalog — `/public/assets/professional/`

## Batch reading
The **professional / tech dimension** of the brand, rendered as clean, photorealistic corporate portraits of Nanda in navy and charcoal power-suits — coding at a monitor, consulting at a city-view desk, a grey-studio headshot, and a downtown-boardroom standing shot. (Note: these read as **AI-generated / composited studio portraits** rather than candid photography — the face is consistent but idealised; they are polished and credible but do not carry the lived warmth of `nanda-culture/`.) They're the right register for the business/tech side that must route users to **mirembemuse.co.za**: cool-to-neutral tones, competent smile, laptops/dashboards/skylines. Most ship a matching **`-transparent.png` cutout** for compositing a floating figure over dark tech gradients or brand panels — cutout quality varies: `nanda-professional-transparent.png` and `nanda-consulting-transparent.png` are clean; **`nanda-posing.png` is a poor/broken cutout** (large black remnants and stray artifacts — utility only). Orientation is square (1:1, 1024px) so plan for a subject-offset crop when you need text space. Use these for AI-engineer, consulting, press, and "the business of Mirembe Muse" sections — pair them against the cultural set to show the full "poet + performer + engineer" range.

## Cutout convention
Where noted, a `-transparent.png` cutout accompanies the `.jpg`. `nanda-posing.png` and `nanda-professional-2-transparent.png` are PNG assets in this folder.
