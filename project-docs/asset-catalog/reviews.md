# Asset Catalog — `public/assets/reviews/`

## Batch reading
This folder is pure social proof for Nanda's poetry, split into two visual formats. The `review-1.jpg` … `review-10.jpg` set are consistently branded 1:1 collage cards: a "My Reviews ❤" black label, a soft pink frame, a vivid painterly illustration of a Black woman reading among tropical flowers (in a pink headwrap or with locs), and a legible typed reader review beside it. The reviews come from an online writing community (usernames like Manche/"Our Chapter", Krystöff, Neema, Raven333, LisaCarol, Rosetta, Poet ljsl, Denis Kutosi) and are genuinely quotable — praising imagery, emotion, and craft. The four `IMG-20260620-WA00xx` files are a different, rawer format: dark WhatsApp screenshots of friends reacting in real time ("I think I've found my new favourite poet/author!!!", "QUEEN snapped", "I'm so proud of her"). All ten collage cards are text-legible and ready as testimonial assets; the WhatsApp shots are authentic but off-brand (dark, phone-UI) and best used sparingly or re-typeset. Note: usernames/timestamps are partly blurred already, but reviewer names are visible — treat as testimonials and confirm consent before publishing.

## Keepers
1. **review-7.jpg** (Neema — "so beautifully composed… how precious of your mother") — emotional, ties to her heritage themes.
2. **review-6.jpg** (Raven333 — "What a poet you are, that was absolutely beautiful") — short, punchy, pull-quote gold.
3. **review-5.jpg** (LisaCarol — "This was simply fabulous… read over-and-over again").
4. **review-1.jpg** / **review-4.jpg** — long, craft-focused reviews for a detailed testimonials page.
5. **IMG-20260620-WA0071.jpg** — the most powerful raw reaction chain, if a re-typeset "what friends say" block is wanted.

**Recommendation:** Re-typeset the best 4–6 quotes into the site's own serif/gold testimonial component rather than posting the collage JPGs full-size — the pink-frame collages read as social-media exports, not premium editorial. Keep the JPGs as source/proof.
