# Asset Catalog — `public/assets/graduation/`

## Batch reading
This is the strongest, most on-brand human folder Nanda has. It documents her Nelson Mandela University graduation (Advanced Diploma) and the family/heritage around it. The visual language is exactly the CreativelyNanda soul palette: a cerise/magenta satin bubble dress under a black academic gown, against lush warm-green tropical foliage (banana palms, cypress, brick paving) and one striking institutional mural. There are two distinct wardrobe/backdrop sets — a full-colour "cerise + gown" set (celebratory, regal) and a softer "white dress + hood" set under a leafy tree (dreamy, contemplative) — plus one exceptional cultural family group shot in full Xhosa traditional dress. Most frames are tall portraits with clean negative space (sky, wall, foliage) that will hold headline or pull-quote text beautifully. Almost every file here is hero- or strong-supporting-grade; there is very little filler.
