## 1. The soul, and the split

CreativelyNanda is the **warm, sensual, regal, cultural SOUL** site. Mirembe Muse
(mirembemuse.co.za) is the **muted, earthy, business-calm** site. Same *architecture*
— every section a full-bleed image, the page unravelling as you scroll — but a
different **key**:

| | Mirembe Muse (business) | CreativelyNanda (soul) |
|---|---|---|
| Palette | muted beige / sage / warm grey | **golden light · rose-red · regal navy+gold · terracotta** |
| Register | restraint, clarity, operational calm | warmth, intimacy, ceremony, sensuality |
| Imagery | textures, neutral lifestyle | **real Nanda** — heritage, poetry, performance, tenderness |

**The one rule that governs everything:** never mix registers on a page. Three tonal
buckets exist and must not bleed into each other where warmth is required:

- **Real-warm** — actual photos/video of Nanda (heritage, poetry, portraits). The soul. Leads everywhere.
- **Illustrated-whimsy** — `nanda-girl/` cartoon "queen" avatars (tiara, outfit swaps). House-of-Roses charm, brand seal, empty states — never where the warm real Nanda is needed.
- **Polished-corporate** — `professional/` AI-generated business portraits + `project-screen-record/` + `work/` builds. The AI-Engineer / Projects / Mirembe hand-off — kept off the poetry soul.

## 4. The placement system (patterns, not a grid)

A flat gallery grid would flatten a woman who is this many things. Instead, a
vocabulary of full-bleed patterns — each photo/video lands where it *sings*:

| Pattern | What it is | Feeds from |
|---|---|---|
| **Full-bleed hero** | Nanda fills the frame; headline set into her negative space | red-corset hero, pond-culture, Lesotho-falls |
| **Layered duo** | warm texture bg + cutout figure floating over it | `professional/` + `nanda-girl/` transparent PNGs over `background-images` keepers |
| **Cinematic band** | edge-to-edge landscape, letterboxed, one line of verse | performance, pergola landscape, culture |
| **Ambient video loop** | muted autoplay clip *as* a section background (not a click-box) | umqombothi, puppy-hearth, book-customer, dinner |
| **Book altar** | the cover on a dark rose-lit stage | `official-cover` over `poetry-lineage-background` |
| **Editorial mosaic** | uneven, rhythmic grid — never flat squares | portraits, candids, culture |
| **Proof strip** | quiet credibility band | radio + reviews + graduation |
| **Ornament layer** | chapter line-art as dividers / margin marks | 6 book chapter drawings as PNGs |

## 5. Page-by-page blueprint (six nav pages, each an unravelling)

### `/` Home — the overture
An unravelling through *all* facets, ending at the Mirembe door.
1. **Threshold hero** — `IMG_20260719_181316` (pond + print) *or* red-corset hero; masthead in the sky negative space.
2. **Poet band** — ambient `book-customer.mp4` behind "She writes in roses"; link → Poetry.
3. **Performer band** — cinematic `nmb-perform-1`, one line of verse.
4. **Heritage band** — ambient `nanda-making-african-beer.mp4` or `WA0018` Lesotho hero; link → About & Lineage.
5. **Builder band** — brief; `nanda-coding` / a project clip; "She also builds" → Mirembe Muse ↗.
6. **Close** — tender selfie / dinner warmth → Contact.

### `/about` About & Lineage — the deepest cultural unravelling
Movements I + IV + V. Umqombothi documentary sequence (`WA0014`→`WA0015` + video),
Basotho/Lesotho heritage, `family-pic-diploma-grad`, the mother's-graduation
role-reversal, `Nsenene Lineage..pdf` as a "lineage" feature. Register: real-warm only.

### `/poetry` Poetry — *(already built — House of Roses)*
Movement II. Book altar (`official-cover`), reader heroes (`book-picture-2/3`),
bougainvillea/candle poetry portraits, chapter-divider ornament system,
`book-signing.mp4` + launch as the "it lives in the world" proof.

### `/gallery` Gallery — the diverse gallery, organised by movement
**Not** a flat grid. An editorial mosaic sectioned by the seven movements, each with
its own full-bleed intro band + ambient video, then a rhythmic uneven grid beneath.
This is where the *range* of Nanda becomes the experience.

### `/blog` Writing — editorial quiet
Quieter paper/quill backgrounds (`poetry-collection-background`,
`Atmospheric reference for ALTERITAS`), pull-quote washes over soft-pink macros,
a single portrait per article header. Text-first, warmth in the margins.

### `/contact` Contact — the warm close
One tender hero (sunlit `nanda-green-1` or a soft selfie) over rose-gold texture
(`download (15)`), gold accents, a single-line invitation. The exhale.
