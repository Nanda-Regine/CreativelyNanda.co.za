# Asset Catalog — `public/assets/radio/`

## Batch reading
This folder is media/press proof: Nanda's MADIBAZ Radio (Nelson Mandela University campus station) interview about her poetry collection *Inside Her Roses*. Three real photographs document the on-air moment in a grey acoustic-panelled studio (mixing desk, boom mics, station banner), and two are decorative "video poster" title cards using a warm coral-and-cream botanical template. The photos are candid and energetic rather than polished — genuine, joyful, human — and crucially the book cover *Inside Her Roses* is clearly held up and legible in two of them, tying the media appearance directly to the product. The two vid-cover cards are utility thumbnails, not real imagery, and match the site's warm floral tone. Overall: authentic press credibility, best used small-to-medium as proof, not as full-bleed heroes.

## Keepers
1. **madiba-radio-3.jpg** — warm hug shot with the *Inside Her Roses* cover fully legible; best single proof image.
2. **madiba-radio-2.jpg** — high-energy "arms up" celebration with the book; great for a lively press strip.
3. **madiba-radio-1.jpg** — the actual on-air-at-the-desk shot; sets the scene.
4. Vid-covers = keep as-is for linking to video, but they're template art, not photography.
