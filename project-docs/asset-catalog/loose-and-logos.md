# Asset Catalog — Loose top-level files + `public/assets/logos/`

## Batch reading
The loose files at the top of `public/assets/` are actually some of the richest, most distinctive imagery on the whole site — they just aren't filed. There are three clusters hiding here: (1) a stunning **Basotho heritage / Lesotho travel** set at a glass gorge viewpoint above a waterfall (Nanda in a Seana Marena blanket and conical grass hat — regal, cinematic, face-obscured-arty); (2) a **spoken-word performance** set on stage in Xhosa umbhaco dress at a mic (proof she performs, not just writes); and (3) more **graduation/family** frames including her *mother's* graduation. There's also a university student group photo, one raw WhatsApp testimonial that belongs with reviews, an abstract painterly wallpaper that's really a background texture, and the Mirembe Muse brand logo. Almost none of this should stay loose — filed correctly it materially strengthens the Culture, Performance, and Milestone sections. Quality is high: several are hero-grade.
