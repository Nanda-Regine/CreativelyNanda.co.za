# Asset Catalog — `/public/assets/nanda-girl/`

## Batch reading
**Important: these are NOT photographs — they are stylised ILLUSTRATED avatar/mascot renderings** of "Nanda" as a cartoon-realistic character (soft cel-shaded, big afro/curls, always wearing a **silver tiara** — the brand's "queen" motif). They form a matched outfit-swap character set (beige / grey / navy / pink power-suits, plus a royal-blue speaking dress and a green-shirt "at the laptop" pose), so they read as one system and are perfect for playful, illustrated UI moments — NOT for anywhere the site needs to feel like real, sensual, cultural Nanda (use `nanda-culture/` there). Every base `.jpg` is a small flat-white-background file that ALSO ships a large **`-transparent.png` cutout** (clean alpha, floating figure) built for compositing over brand backgrounds. `pink-suit.png` and `logo-circle` are PNG-only. The tiara + gold-accent + storybook rendering suit the "House of Roses" whimsy, brand mascot, empty-states, avatars, and marketing coverlines. Colours are on-brand (cream, navy, royal blue, blush pink, gold) but the flat cartoon finish is lower-fidelity than the premium photographic look — deploy as accent/character assets, not as premium heroes.

## Cutout convention
For every entry below, a matching `<name>-transparent.png` exists (large, clean alpha cutout for compositing a floating figure over any background) UNLESS noted. `pink-suit` ships only as PNG (already transparent); `logo-circle` ships jpg + transparent png but is a framed badge, not a cutout figure.

## Notes for the art director
- All figures share the **tiara + afro** identity — consistent mascot system; mix freely.
- Because they're illustrations, keep them in **playful / illustrated / whimsical** contexts (House of Roses whimsy, empty states, step UIs, avatars). Do **not** substitute them where the design calls for the real, warm, cultural photographic Nanda.
- Transparent PNGs are the working assets (composite over gradients, gold texture, rose motifs); the flat jpgs are just previews.
