# Asset Catalog — `/public/assets/nanda-culture/`

## Batch reading
This is the emotional heart of the whole site: **real, un-retouched photographs of Nanda** living her culture. Two distinct sub-batches. First, a bright golden-hour "garden" set (palm trees, stone pergola, a mirror pond, a shweshwe/Xhosa-print mandala plaza) shot in warm South African winter sun — Nanda in African-print trousers with long honey-brown braids, regal and grounded. Second, the true heritage documentary gold: **making traditional African sorghum beer (umqombothi)** by candle/tungsten light in an ochre-walled home, and an **isiXhosa/Sotho beaded regalia** portrait, plus a Sotho-blanket-and-sun-hat moment on a Drakensberg/Golden Gate mountain walkway. These are the images mirembemuse's clinical tech aesthetic can never have — they carry lineage, ritual, ancestry, sensual warmth. Orientation skews portrait (phone 3:4) so they slot beautifully into full-bleed mobile hero washes and layered "unravelling" scroll panels; the two landscape frames (pergola, jungle giant) are your desktop banners. Colour story is exactly the brand: golden light, terracotta/ochre, red-rose accents, deep African-print reds and blues.
