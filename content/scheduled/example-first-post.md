## The Reality We Face

As a South African freelancer, load-shedding isn't just an inconvenience—it's a daily puzzle we have to solve. After years of adapting, I've developed strategies that keep me productive even during Stage 6.

## My Essential Toolkit

### Hardware

- **UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply)**: My lifeline. A good UPS gives me 3-4 hours of laptop power.
- **Power bank**: For phones and tablets
- **LTE router with battery**: WiFi doesn't stop when Eskom does

### Apps & Services

- **EskomSePush**: Know your schedule before it happens
- **Offline-capable tools**: Notion, VS Code, Figma all work offline
- **Cloud sync**: Everything syncs when power returns

## Structuring Your Day

I've learned to work WITH the load-shedding schedule, not against it:

1. **High-focus work** during guaranteed power times
2. **Calls and meetings** scheduled around slots
3. **Admin and planning** can happen on mobile during outages

## The Mindset Shift

The biggest change wasn't technical—it was mental. I stopped seeing load-shedding as an obstacle and started seeing it as forced focus time.

No Netflix. No endless scrolling. Just work or rest.
