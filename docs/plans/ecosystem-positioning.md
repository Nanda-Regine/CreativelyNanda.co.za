# Ecosystem positioning: plan of record (20 Nov 2026)

Goal: strong, earned authority for Nandawula Regine and Mirembe Muse in the global market, built on proof a stranger can check.

## The claim
Nandawula Regine builds AI and digital platforms for African institutions and communities, and works with a network of partners in skills, education, creative production and events.

Authority rule: every claim needs a number, a live link, a screen, or a named partner behind it. No superlatives that are not measured.

## Authority levers, in order
1. Hiring and partnership block on the Forge: email, CV, LinkedIn, "work with the network" route for funders and for clients.
2. Ecosystem page on mirembemuse.co.za (separate repo): the four partners, who does what, three-step "how we work together".
3. Outcome numbers on each case file (hours saved, members, events run, payments processed), each approved by the client or partner and sourced through a Claim.
4. One client or partner quote per case, with consent.
5. A joint Joko & Co + Taek Two project to give the last two partners real evidence.
6. Forge essay: building ecosystems in Africa, not just products (ISD as worked example). No em dashes.
7. Poet press kit split from the company press kit at /press.
8. One shared fact file for both sites so counts never conflict.
