# Mrs Mthembu (SME owner): I found the way to the business side, but only because I was looking hard; this first screen is a poet's cover, not a builder's.
Entry: https://creativelynanda.co.za/ · Device: Android 412x915, Chrome, fibre · Goal: can Nanda build for my business, cost, how to reach her · Outcome: ACHIEVED WITH FRICTION (reached Mirembe Muse; pricing and contact not confirmed within this walk)

## The walk (in character)
1. Google gets me to the homepage. Blank navy for about 3 seconds, then a woman's portrait fades in. I see "CREATIVELY NANDA", "Inside her roses", "Spoken word", "Nine generations", "Amahlubi & Buganda". Poetry. Am I in the right place? Not obviously.
2. Small text at the right, mid-screen: "SHE ALSO BUILDS: MIREMBE MUSE ↗". It's tiny, but it's the first hint she builds anything. I tap it.
3. (Checked the alternative too.) The hamburger menu opens a long Story/Poetry list, and a gold button at the bottom: "Visit Mirembe Muse ↗", under "FOR BUSINESS & TECH". That one I'd have found only after scrolling past poetry links. Good wording, poor position.
4. Mirembe Muse loads in about 7 seconds. "Where your business becomes one calm system." Plain, about me, with a "Build With Us" button. Better. But a cookie box sits on top of that button and its text overlaps, so I can't read or tap it cleanly.
5. Further down: "I run a business: show my path". That is the right door. A WhatsApp button is visible bottom-left. I trust that more than a form.
6. I did not confirm a price or "from R…" in this walk, so I can't say I got my cost answer.
